\subsection{{LMPool: KV-Aware Routing and NVLink Transfer}\hfill Apr 2026 - Jul 2026}
\begin{zitemize}
\item \textbf{Architecture:} Split the runtime into a launcher, one global control plane, and one data plane per GPU. The control plane tracks versioned block tables, capacity, reservations, and placement metadata while each worker retains physical ownership of its KV cache.
\item \textbf{Strategies:} Routing: The global scheduler combines missing prefill, queue/decode work, reclaim pressure, and free capacity. Transfer: Cross-GPU moves use a prepare-execute-publish-finalize transaction with source hash/generation checks, destination reservations, and publication-before-visibility.
\item \textbf{Experiments:} Experiment on six RTX 3090 GPUs with five repeated runs, 5x shared-prefix routing improved Qwen3-0.6B/1.7B throughput by 11.8\%/21.3\% and reduced mean TTFT by 18.6\%/25.2\% versus round robin. Load skew completed three placements and copied 87 blocks. \href{https://0x0addc001.github.io/LMPool/}{Project page}
\end{zitemize}
